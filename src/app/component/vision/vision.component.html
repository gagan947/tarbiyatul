Our Mission

The mission of TIAG is to provide a K-4 education that expands globally through self-paced online learning. We focus on providing excellence in education while nurturing Muslim character in an enjoyable and personalized learning environment that intrigue and inspire.

Our Vision

Our vision is that children leave our school with:

  • A set of spiritual and moral values -- the fear of Allah, honesty, respect and integrity
  • A complement of basic skills -- educational, physical, and social.
  • Strong self-esteem and high personal expectations
  • To have developed a strong sense of community service
